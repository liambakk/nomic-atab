# First handoff checklist

Use this checklist for one selected company and workflow. Complete the templates where the information is available. Ben can send the answers and source files directly to Liam if that is easier.

## Agree the starting point

- [ ] Ben recommends a company and workflow, including whether the platform is live and has real records.
- [ ] Ben and Liam agree which company and workflow to investigate.
- [ ] Ben identifies the Atab technical contact and business reviewer.
- [ ] Preparation effort and any cost are discussed and agreed before the work is incurred.
- [ ] Permission to share the first material with Liam is recorded, including any restrictions.
- [ ] Ben proposes a handoff date and walkthrough time.

## Prepare the material

- [ ] Complete the [company profile](templates/company-profile.md) for the selected source.
- [ ] Describe the [workflow](templates/workflow.md), including decisions, constraints and exceptions.
- [ ] Complete a [case record](templates/case.md) for each available case. Aim for one routine case and two involving investigation, a decision or a correction.
- [ ] Preserve source records showing the initial situation, actions, corrections and outcome. Keep information available during the work separate from later knowledge.
- [ ] Identify the person who can assess each result and any acceptable alternatives.
- [ ] Provide existing field definitions, action descriptions, business rules and automation notes using [system notes](system-notes/README.md).
- [ ] Index the source files in [data-manifest.csv](data-manifest.csv). Preserve timestamps, identifiers, units and relationships, or document consistent replacements.
- [ ] Record missing information, its status and the person who can help.

## Deliver and review

- [ ] Share the source folder privately with Liam and confirm he can open it.
- [ ] Complete the [permission record](permissions.md) for the material and uses currently cleared. Leave later uses pending until confirmed.
- [ ] Link each case to its source file references and include a short file index.
- [ ] Flag whether the starting state and sequence are reconstructable, and explain any gaps.
- [ ] Hold the walkthrough with Ben, the relevant Atab contact and the business reviewer where needed.
- [ ] Liam records whether the material is usable for an initial sample and which questions remain.

## What happens after the first review

Liam builds the evaluations or environment, including actions, resets, grading and model tests. The business reviewer helps verify the rules and valid outcomes. Ben and Liam agree any further records, ongoing support or commercial terms before committing to additional work or delivery.

Completing the checklist records a handoff for assessment; it does not establish lab acceptance, a purchase or agreed commercial licensing.
